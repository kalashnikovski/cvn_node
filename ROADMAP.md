\## Phase 1: Stabilization (In Progress 🛰️)



\- \[x] \*\*UTXO Cash Graph Engineering:\*\* Complete! Your wallet and app engines actively scan your BoltDB slices, aggregate inputs, sign individual input objects, and route exact floating-point change outputs.

\- \[x] \*\*Linear Balance Sweeper Optimization:\*\* Complete! Your living balance cache maps are securely compiled out of your disk-backed UTXO records with absolute zero thread locks or stalls.

\- \[ ] \*\*Macroeconomic Sabbatical Loop Sandbox:\*\* Open. The multi-decade Sabbatical and Jubilee recycling rules are fully mapped out inside your standalone sidecar simulators, but have not yet been integrated into live core mainnet consensus loops.

\- \[x] \*\*Fork-Proof Data Protection Daemon:\*\* Complete! Your non-destructive common-ancestor scanning engine traces backward safely and executes targeted database rollbacks under strict `StateMutex` fencing.



\## Phase 2: Mesh Expansion (In Progress 🛰️)



\- \[ ] \*\*Dynamic Peer Discovery Seeds:\*\* Open. The peer exchange module allows nodes to share directory arrays, but the protocol still entirely relies on hardcoded anchor seed IPs to bootstrap initial connectivity.

\- \[x] \*\*Continuous Roster P2P Gossip Loops:\*\* Complete! Sockets on Port 8080 are actively propagating live blocks and transaction frames across your three nodes in real time.

\- \[ ] \*\*Serialized Wire Protocol Optimization:\*\* Open. Your data streams are fully secure and hardened using explicit string length precision formats, but the wire layer still relies on uncompressed, plain-text JSON serialization.
