Covenant Standard (CVN)

A proof-of-work blockchain written from scratch, built for fair, open mining on ordinary home computers. No pre-mine, no founder's share, no investor sale.

Status: in testing. Nothing has launched.

The rules are being rehearsed on a small development network, which was started afresh on 27 November 2026. The public test network, the CVN Testnet, has not started; its start will be announced in advance as a Bitcoin block height, and everyone begins from zero at that block.

Coins on every test network have no value and carry over to nothing. Nothing is for sale here, and nobody is asked for money.

The design in brief

Block time45 seconds; difficulty adjusts on every block
MiningRandomX, tuned for home processors
Block reward50 CVN, halving every 7 years for 49 years
Total supply486,727,500 CVN, and no more
FeesVoluntary; a transfer with no fee is allowed. Every fee goes to the miner of the block, and a fifth of every block is closed to transfers that pay one
The seventh yearA fifth of any coin left unmoved for 7 years is set aside. Its owner has 7 more years to take it back, simply by moving the coin or renewing it in the wallet. What nobody comes for then goes into a common vault, which pays it out little by little to those who mine.
Pre-mineNone. The first block pays nothing to anyone.
LedgerOpen: every address and amount can be seen by anyone

The full rules, and what the design does not do, are in the design paper.

A fair start

  • No coins have been sold, promised or set aside for anyone, and none will be. Every coin there will ever be is paid by the rules to whoever mines a block.
  • The start is tied to a Bitcoin block that does not exist yet when it is announced. Nobody can know a future Bitcoin block, so nobody can mine early, including the people who wrote the software.
  • The seed nodes do not mine. They keep the chain and pass blocks on, and have no say over the rules.
  • Every download can be checked. Each release lists its SHA-256 sums, is signed as built from its source, and can be rebuilt by anyone to the same bytes.

The whole statement, and what outside reviewers found when they tested it against the code, is in the README.

Staying safe

  • This site's addresses all end in covenantstandard.online. The programs come only from the project's releases on GitHub.
  • The address covenantstandard.com belongs to someone else and has nothing to do with this project. The project sends no email: an email that claims to come from it is not from the project.
  • Nobody from the project will ever ask for your private key or your 24 recovery words. Anyone who has them has your coins.
